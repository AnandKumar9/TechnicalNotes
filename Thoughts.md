##### Dec 26th, 2022

Its now been 6.5 years that I have been at Capital One. Joining Capital One and getting through the first few months there is something which I really feel I had handled very well then, and was an important step forward in my career. In the initial years, I operated as an iOS expert and the years rolled by. Trying to ship features flawlessly, being bankable, and working for my next promotion and appraisal. But now if I see, more or less I am still what I was 6.5 years back. An iOS expert, that's all. I could have used these years to be more verstile than that, but I was too busy with feature work to become that.

Here is what I now look for. To become actually knowledgable and well versed with things other than iOS. Android, web development, back-end development, machine learning, and so on. In fact, all kinds of things and every new popular thing I hear about I should try to become familiar with it. I of course don't need to be equally proficient in all of them but should try to have some understanding in each. And then to become really good in iOS. To know all that's happening and new, but then also be really deep in advanced topics. To clearly be a standout exponent who actually understands things very well.

Sometimes pursuing such excellence can be daunting. How do I even begin to comprehend such sophisticated sounding things. But then anything is sophisticated only until you haven't known it. Once I find the basics, everything is simple. Also, nearly no one is actually an all-knowing genius expert. Most of the times they learned just enough to be able to speak confidently, don't know much outside it, and some times are just faking it. Don't be bothered or daunted. Remember, no one knows everything. Just be confident in your own abilities.

In addition, it will also help to know more about data structures and algorithms. But is it needed already, there might be too many things to tackle if I try that too. It will be better if I give myself sometime to upskill, and then once I want to actually start looking outside that I start brushing up data structures and algorithms too.

Work hard, quite hard. Make sustained progress, all the time. Every week, and with a plan. I owe it to myself.
