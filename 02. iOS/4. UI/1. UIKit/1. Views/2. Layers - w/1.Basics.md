What is a layer and what are various kinds of layers -

A layer is something just like a view and is represented by CALayer. Its a rectangular area. It manages its visual content and also has information about its geometry (position, transform, etc.). 

It conforms to CAMediaTiming protocol, thereby containing information about its animation duration as well.

Every view has a backing layer. A layer can be created afresh too (i.e. without a view) but when a layer is created by a view, the view automatically assigns itself as the layer’s delegate and that should not be changed. 

CALayerDelegate is the protocol for a layer’s delegate. It has a few methods for reporting events related to the drawing of the layer and its sublayers.

There are several special kinds of layers, i.e. different subclasses of CALayer. Here are all the subclasses of CALayer.
