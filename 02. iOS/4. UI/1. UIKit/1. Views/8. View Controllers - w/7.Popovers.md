With a popover controller, the content is presented in a popover which is a visual layer that floats above the app’s window. Popovers are capable of presenting most types of view controllers.

Popovers are dismissed automatically when the user taps outside the popover view.

When presenting a popover, UIPopoverArrowDirectionAny constant gives UIKit the maximum flexibility in positioning and resizing the popover.
