iPhone - iPhone 6S and 6S Plus.
* Screen size remains the same for both.
* Processor and camera have been bumped up.
* New 3D Touch gesture made available, it will work on home screen as well as within the app.
* TouchID will be double as fast as now.

When 3D Touch is done on an app icon home screen, a pop up with options will come and it will also be possible to preview some app content.

Apple TV -
* Siri enabled remote. Siri can answer specific questions about movies and TV shows.
* A new App Store has been introduced for Apple TV along with a new OS called tvOS. The TV will have apps.
* Remote also now has a glass touch surface for browsing through content on the Apple TV display. It will even have accelerometer and gyroscope which could be heavily used in game apps that could now be build for Apple TV.

iPad - iPad Pro introduced
* Will have a 12.9 inch display which will be a bigger than a 11 inch MacBook Air and as heavy as the generation 1 iPad which was released 5 years back in 2010.
* It will of course have much more powerful hardware.
* It gets two new accessories, a smart keyboard and an Apple Pencil. Apple Pencil is a nifty stylus. iPad Pro now becomes what Wacom was until now and likely better.
* iPad Pro will also make split screen apps lot more useful. There will really be a lot of space for two different apps to together show up with enough real estate for both.
