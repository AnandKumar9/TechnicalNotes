##### New Macs

Introduced 15 inch MacBook Air. MagSafe charging, 2 Thunderbolt ports,1 headphone jack. 1080 p camera, 3 microphones. 18 hr battery life. M2 chip

M2 chips - M2, M2 Pro, M2 Max, M2 Ultra

Introduced M2 Ultra. Two M2 Max tied together. 76 core GPU. 32 core neural engine. 192 GB Unified memory. Supports 6 Pro display XDRs

Introduced Mac Pro with Apple Silicon. Can ingest 24 4K camera feeds and encode them to max res real time
