What happens in case of a view controller having several sub-views, do all of them need to have restoration identifiers specified separately?

When the home button is double pressed, all that comes up is the list of recently used apps. It does not necessarily mean that all those apps are running at that time in background. In fact, its not even an accurate list of recently used apps (as the user may manually remove anything from there by swiping up) or even an accurate list of apps currently being run (an app that has been launched in background by the system will not show up there). Its just an indicative list, nothing precise.

Just by looking at that list, it is impossible to tell what apps are currently running or even what apps were last used.
