Snap behavior is programmatically a very simple thing and all it does is to move a dynamic item (probably its center) to the specified point and then quickly oscillate around it before it settles down. It gives the appearance of a spring like effect.

```
self.snapBehavior = UISnapBehavior.init(item: self.view1, snapToPoint: self.view.center)
```

damping property specifies the amount of oscillation. It ranges from 0.0 to 1.0 with 0.5 being the default.
