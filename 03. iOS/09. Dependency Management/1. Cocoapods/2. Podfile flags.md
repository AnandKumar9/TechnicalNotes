##### use_frameworks! -


Causes the dependencies to be added as frameworks instead of static libraries.

###### How I tested it

*** | *** | *** | ***
--- | --- | --- | ---
Added 3 frameworks with flag `use_frameworks!` in `podfile`. <br><br>Hence, as shown below the concerned target has everything included as `Pods_TestApp1.framework`. | Adding the dependencies as frameworks.<br> <br> As expected, they show up as frameworks in the `Pods.xcproj` portion of the project navigator. | Added 2 frameworks without flag `use_frameworks!` in `podfile`.<br><br> Hence, as shown below the concerned target has everything included as `libPods_TestApp2.a`. | Adding the dependencies as libraries.<br><br> This causes them to  show up as libraries in the pod portion of the project navigator.
