### GeometryReader

A container view that defines its content as a function of its own size and coordinate space.

### GeometryProxy

A proxy for access to the size and coordinate space (for anchor resolution) of the container view.

### GeometryEffect

An effect that changes the visual appearance of a view, largely without changing its ancestors or descendants.

### Anchor

An opaque value derived from an anchor source and a particular view.

### Angle

A geometric angle whose value you access in either radians or degrees.

### Misc. types

`UnitPoint`, `Axis`, `Edge`, `HorizontalEdge`, `VerticalEdge`, `RoundedCornerStyle`, `CoordinateSpace`, `ProjectionTransform`, `VectorArithmetic`.
