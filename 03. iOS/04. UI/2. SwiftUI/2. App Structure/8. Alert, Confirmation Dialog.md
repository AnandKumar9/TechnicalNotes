#### Confirmation

Its the SwiftUI equivalent of UIAlertController in actionSheet mode. It brings up various option buttons from bottom (think this was in UIKit too). Its done using methods such as **confirmationDialog(_:isPresented:titleVisibility:actions:)**. <br> [Reference](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/swiftui/view/confirmationdialog(_:ispresented:titlevisibility:actions:))
