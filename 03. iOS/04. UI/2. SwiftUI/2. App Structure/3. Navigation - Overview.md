##### Sections in [Navigation](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/swiftui/navigation) homepage (~iOS 18, 2024)

Presenting views in columns - `NavigationSplitView`, `NavigationSplitViewVisibility`, `NavigationLink` <br>Stacking views in one column - `NavigationSatck`, `NavigationView`<br>Managing column collapse - `NavigationSplitViewColumn` <br>Setting titles for navigation content <br>Configuring the navigation bar - `NavigationBarItem`<br>Configuring the sidebar - `SidebarRowSize`<br>Presenting views in tabs - `TabView`, `Tab`, `TabRole`, `TabSection`<br>Configuring a tab bar - `AdaptableTabBarPlacement`, `TabBarPlacement`<br>Configuring a tab - `TabPlacement`, `TabContentBuilder`<br>Enabling tab customization - `TabViewCustomization`, `TabCustomizationBehavior` <br>Displaying views in multiple panes - `HSplitView`, `VSplitView` (both are **macOS** only) <br>

iOS 16 onwards, use `NavigationStack` and `NavigationSplitView` instead of `NavigationView`.
