Sequence of things in AuthHub test iOS app that connects to GitHub using OAuth -

[GitHub's OAuth](https://docs.github.com/en/apps/oauth-apps/building-oauth-apps/authorizing-oauth-apps)

signIn - happens in webview

access_token
user
repos

When token is already there - 
Make URLSession call to - GetUserDetails
Make URLSession call to - GetRepos

After signing out, resigning -
Make ASWebAuthenticationSession call to - SignIn URL
Make URLSession call to - GetAccessToken
Make URLSession call to - GetUserDetails
Make URLSession call to - GetUserDetails

After reinstalling app - 
Make URLSession call to - GetUserDetails 
(Failed to get user, or there is no valid/active session: otherError)
Make ASWebAuthenticationSession call to - SignIn URL
Make URLSession call to - GetAccessToken
Make URLSession call to - GetUserDetails
TAP SIGN-IN AGAIN -
Make ASWebAuthenticationSession call to - SignIn URL
Make URLSession call to - GetAccessToken
Make URLSession call to - GetUserDetails
Make URLSession call to - GetRepos
