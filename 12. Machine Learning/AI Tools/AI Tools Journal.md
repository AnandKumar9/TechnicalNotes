**GPT** - Generative Pre-Trained Transformer. Basically text and image generation.

Evolution (becomes more knowledgable and fast) - 

```
GPT4 > GPT4-Turbo > GPT-4Omni (aka GPT-4o), GPT-4o Mini 
```

GPT-4o supports text, vision (i.e. image), and audio input+output. GPT-4o Mini supports test and vision. For simple asks however, usually GPT-4o Mini is faster than GPT-4o. As of early 2025, openAI website uses GPT-4o and Mini is accessible only through its API.
