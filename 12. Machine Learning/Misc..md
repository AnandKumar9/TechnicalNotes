What really is Articifial Intelligence. The way I understand it is that when software becomes unreally human in its sophistication, such that the kind of things its able to do are far more advanced than what's the norm its called `Artifical Intelligence`.

There are several branches of AI, Machine Learning (ML) too is just a branch of AI.

1. Computer Vision - Identifying and analysing images
2. Fuzzy Logic - Rules for decisioning yes/no
3. Expert Systems - Focuses on specific areas
4. Robotics - Controls hardware
5. Machine Learning - Mathematical decisioning/prediction based on enormous amounts of data
6. Neural Networks/Deep Learning
7. Natural Language Processing - Understanding text and voice
